2. Strategy parameters

Rule: Buy when today's close falls at least the trigger % from yesterday's close and the cooldown has expired. During cooldown, buy anyway if today's close is at least the override % below the most recent purchase price. Every purchase resets the cooldown and reference price. The corpus is a hard ceiling and partial purchases are not allowed.
